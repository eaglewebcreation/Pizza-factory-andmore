Our story

For the love of family, food & community

Homemade goodness can be found at The Pizza Factory & More. We specialize in pizza, but we offer so many other choices, and everything is made with fresh ingredients.

We make our dough every day. Our ranch dressing, garlic parmesan sauce and white pizza sauce are made from scratch, and so are our sweet treats. Our oven-toasted hoagies are made on fresh bread baked right here.

We're a family-run kitchen, and we cook like it. Whether you're grabbing a pizza for movie night, feeding a team after practice or sitting down for lunch, we're glad you're here.
