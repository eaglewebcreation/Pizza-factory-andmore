Questions & answers

Where is The Pizza Factory & More?

We're at 490 Shoshone Ave in Lovell, Wyoming 82431, in the Big Horn Basin. It's an easy drive from Cowley, Byron and Deaver.

What are your hours?

Monday 4 to 8 PM. Tuesday through Friday 11 AM to 2 PM and 4 to 8 PM. Saturday 4 to 8 PM. Closed Sunday. Holiday hours can change, so give us a call if you're unsure.

Can I order ahead for pickup?

Yes. Call (307) 548-2206 and we'll have your order hot and ready when you get here. Online ordering is on the way.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Yes. We offer a gluten-free crust on medium specialty and ultimate pizzas, gluten-free chicken strips and a gluten-free rice bowl. Let us know when you order.

What size pizza should I get?

An individual pizza is made for one person and also comes as a flat bread. A medium is 8 slices and feeds 2 to 4. A large is 12 slices and feeds 4 to 6.

What do you make from scratch?

Our pizza dough is made fresh every day. Our ranch dressing, garlic parmesan sauce, white pizza sauce and sweet treats are homemade, and our hoagies are made on bread baked in-house.

Is there a good place for pizza near Bighorn Canyon?

Lovell is the gateway town to the Bighorn Canyon National Recreation Area. Grab a pizza or a few toasted hoagies to go before you head out to the canyon.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Try the Veggie pizza, a cheese pizza with your favorite veggie toppings, a garden salad, breadsticks or any of our fried sides.

Do you charge a credit card fee?

We charge an additional 3% for credit card use to help offset processing costs. Debit cards are not surcharged.

Can I dine in?

Yes. We offer dine-in and takeout, with high chairs for little ones and parking on site.

Still have a question? Call us at (307) 548-2206.
